For my ENGL 1301 class, I got to present my "literacy narrative" to the whole class, I did not really expect it to be this much of a personal project, but looking back, oh it definitely was.

This was MY presentation

[~]$ whoami

But let us free of any regrets, i think I do have a sense of pride on my work, what I came up with physically and what I wanted to reflect on the digital world. Because yes, I separated this presentation in two.

Since I did reflect a lot into what I believe is the global path of learning, the digital world symbolizes what I see as the way we all learn, it has a little hint of my growth, of course, but it truly is the means through we explore and find passion in what truly makes us each unique yet unified, as you may guess, the physical presentation (the objects) are those means, apart from a couple of symbolisms.

TL;DR: The website is a narrative reflection on literacy, the presentation is how I learned throughout MY LIFE. (global vs personal)

The presentation you see next to this window is what I showed to the public in the classroom, a bunch of random stuff, ain't it? Well yes, but I put a meaning into them, I promise!
